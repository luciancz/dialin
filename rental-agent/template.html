How the numbers are worked out

Source. OnTheMarket only for this run. OpenRent showed a human-verification puzzle to the automated browser, so no OpenRent listings are included. Rightmove and Zoopla were not searched.

Walking time. Routed on-foot distance to the South Kensington campus at 4.8 km/h. Listing pins are placed where the agent placed them, which can be the street, not the door.

Expected rent. Asking rent less an assumed negotiation discount: 1.5% for large agents, 3% for independents, plus 1% if listed over two weeks, 1.5% if already reduced, 1% if offers are invited, minus 1% if new this week, capped at 8%. These are assumptions, not measured outcomes.

Niceness. A model reviewed each listing's photos and description against eight weighted criteria: appliances 15, fixtures and fittings 15, furnishings 10, condition 15, light and layout 15, space 15, outdoor space and floor level 10, EPC and street 5. Furnishings is left out for unfurnished flats. Photos were reviewed at thumbnail size, so appliance brands are often not legible.

Value. 50 means priced as expected, higher means cheaper than expected.
